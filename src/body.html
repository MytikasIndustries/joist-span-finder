Before you spec.

  • Spans are for uniform floor loads. Concentrated loads, openings, cantilevers and continuous spans other than two equal spans need an engineer.
  • The top (compression) flange is braced along its full length by sheathing fastened to it. Joists are braced against rotation at every support, and flanges are fastened to the support.
  • Every support assumes 3-1/2" of bearing. Shorter bearing changes the web stiffener requirement and may reduce capacity.
  • Two equal spans: enter each span, measured from an end support to the center support. The joist runs continuous over the center support.
  • Total-load deflection is limited to L/240 in every table, with live-load deflection at L/360 or L/480 as selected.
  • With sheathing on the top flange only, install bridging on the bottom flange at no more than 8 ft on center.
  • Web punchouts near a support may need reinforcement.

Where the numbers come from.

Allowable spans are from the ClarkDietrich “Cold-Formed Structural Framing Products Technical Design Guide” (Pub. No. CD-STR-TechGuide, effective 06/01/24), floor joist tables pp. 63 to 88, designed to AISI S100-16 (2020) w/S2-20. It is the industry reference our engineers use. Joist weights are from the same guide, pp. 13 to 17.

Mytikas Steel joists are roll-formed from 50 ksi steel in Florence, Colorado, and meet or exceed the ClarkDietrich sections these tables are based on: 2" flange, 5/8" lip, 54, 68 and 97 mil.
